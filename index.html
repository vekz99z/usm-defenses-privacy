Privacy Policy · Last Updated: September 24, 2026

USM Defenses ("the Bot," "we," "us," or "our") is a Discord bot designed to provide moderation, administrative, utility, and other features for Discord servers.

By using USM Defenses, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

USM Defenses may collect or process limited information necessary to provide its features, including:

  • Discord User IDs
  • Discord Server/Guild IDs
  • Discord Channel IDs
  • Discord Role IDs
  • Discord usernames and display names
  • Commands and interactions sent to the Bot
  • Moderation-related information, such as warnings, mutes, kicks, bans, or other administrative actions
  • Configuration settings created by server administrators

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as passwords or payment information unless a specific feature explicitly requires it and clearly informs users.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide and operate Bot features
  • Perform moderation and administrative functions
  • Store server configuration settings
  • Maintain moderation records when applicable
  • Prevent abuse, spam, or misuse of the Bot
  • Troubleshoot technical problems
  • Improve the Bot's reliability and functionality

3. Data Storage

Some information may be stored in databases or other systems used to operate USM Defenses.

We attempt to retain only information that is reasonably necessary for the Bot's functionality.

Server administrators may be able to configure, remove, or reset certain data through available Bot commands.

4. Discord Information

USM Defenses operates through Discord and may receive information made available to the Bot through Discord's API and permissions.

Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. USM Defenses does not control how Discord independently processes information.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

We do not intentionally share collected information with third parties except when reasonably necessary to:

  • Operate the Bot and its infrastructure
  • Comply with applicable law or legal requests
  • Protect the Bot, its users, or its infrastructure from abuse or security threats

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by USM Defenses. However, no online service can guarantee that information will always remain completely secure.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy. When information is no longer required, it may be deleted or removed from our systems.

8. Data Deletion Requests

To request deletion of information associated with your Discord account or server, contact us using the information below.

Please include your Discord User ID and/or Server ID when submitting a request so we can identify the relevant information.

9. Children's Privacy

USM Defenses does not intentionally collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to providing its services. Users should follow Discord's minimum age requirements and applicable laws in their location.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will be updated. Continued use of USM Defenses after changes are published constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

By using USM Defenses, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
